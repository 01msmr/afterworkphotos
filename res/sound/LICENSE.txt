The lift's music, res/sound/jazz-*.mp3
--------------------------------------

Seven pieces from the album "Teh Jazzes" (2017) by Kevin MacLeod, chosen
for being quiet and unhurried and each a different colour:

  jazz-backbay.mp3    Backbay Lounge
  jazz-bossa.mp3      Bossa Antigua
  jazz-apero.mp3      Apero Hour
  jazz-crinoline.mp3  Crinoline Dreams
  jazz-noel.mp3       Nouvelle Noel
  jazz-deuces.mp3     Deuces
  jazz-smooth.mp3     Smooth Lovin'

Music by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com)
Licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Each file here is the first 90 seconds of its track, faded in and out,
mono at 32 kHz — it is heard through the wall of a lift cabin, and the
whole set then weighs less than one of the gallery's photographs.

The lift's own sounds (bell.mp3, call.mp3, motor.mp3, ride.mp3) are not
part of this and carry their own provenance.

The guard's voice, res/sound/guard/*.mp3
----------------------------------------

Not a recording of anyone: the lines were spoken by macOS's own speech
synthesiser (the `say` command, voice "Daniel", en-GB) and trimmed to
small mono files. One file per line the guard has; the text of each is in
res/gallery/guard.js, and the file is named for it.

Made this way because a headset browser may carry no speech engine of its
own, which left the guard silent (2026-09-12).
